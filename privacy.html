Ghostmarks Privacy Policy

App: Ghostmarks: Tic Tac Toe XO · Last updated: 31 October 2026

This policy explains what information Ghostmarks uses and why. Ghostmarks is made by Balakrishna ("we").

Information the game keeps on your device

Your settings, game statistics, daily puzzle streak and theme choice are saved only on your own device. We do not receive them, and they are deleted if you uninstall the app or clear the browser's site data.

Information we collect

We do not ask for your name, email, location, contacts or photos, and we do not run our own servers that collect your data.

Advertising (mobile app)

The mobile app shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads and prevent fraud, Google may collect information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location, device and app details, and how you interact with ads. On iPhone, you will be asked whether to allow tracking, and you can change this later in Settings. Where the law requires it, you will be asked for consent first. You can learn how Google uses this information at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites, and you can reset or limit your advertising ID in your phone's settings.

Website version

If you play on our website or on a game portal, that website or portal may use its own cookies and ads under its own privacy policy.

Children

Ghostmarks is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Your choices

  • Uninstall the app or clear site data to delete everything stored on your device.
  • Change ad tracking and ad personalisation in your phone's privacy settings.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the date above.
